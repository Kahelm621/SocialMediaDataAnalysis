Outliers

A video is listed when its views are at least 3× the median of that channel's videos in the latest snapshot. The pool is your videos, enabled competitors, and a small sample of channels that were on the trending chart. It is not every video on YouTube.

Where the numbers came from

    What the words mean

    Engagement rate is likes plus comments plus shares, divided by views. If a platform does not send shares, those are left out. YouTube's public API does not include shares. On X, the "views" number is impressions.

    Views per day is how many views you gained between the two newest snapshots, divided by the days between those snapshots.

    YouTube attention for a keyword is the combined views of the top search results, not an exact count of every video with that phrase. The opportunity score uses that median plus channel size. It is an estimate, not search volume.

    Lifetime pace is views divided by hours since publish. Snapshot pace is the change between the last two saves. Neither is a live views-per-hour feed.

    Trending is the official mostPopular chart for a region and category. How long a video stays is how many of your saved snapshots still include it.

    X hashtag volume is the recent post count when the counts endpoint allows it. If X refuses, the collector saves a small recent sample and labels it that way.

    TikTok uses the official Display API (your approved account) and Research API (hashtags, after approval). Nothing is scraped.

    Hours use the timezone in config/tracked.yaml. It is UTC until you change it.
